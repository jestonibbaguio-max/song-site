Step 2

Create or update your CV and follow the guidelines below:

  • It should be written in third person point of view.
  • It should have complete details including:
    • Date duration in the project.
    • Role and technologies used in the project (e.g. Frontend developer (ReactJS)).
    • Your responsibilities and or achievements on each project.
  • Start the sequence of your previous experiences with the most recent on the top.
  • Mentioned all the skills you have and have used in projects. Even the trainings that you've completed.
  • Include all the trainings or activities that you are currently doing or have completed during your stay in Song Bench.
  • Project name/Client name should be mentioned.
  • Insert a picture. Make sure to provide appropriate and professional looking profile photo.
  • Make sure the CV file does not exceed 200kb and the file name is formatted to <EID>_OneSliderCV.
