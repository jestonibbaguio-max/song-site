Check your Workstation Compliance and Admin Rights

Don't miss out on important compliance actions as well as admin rights privilege to ensure that your capability building and bench journey continues smoothly.

✓ Task Completed
Step 1

Check your workstation compliance by accessing the Devices & Equipments page in the Accenture Support site by Accenture Support > Devices tab.

🗗 Accenture Support: Devices & Equipment
Step 2

Your current workstation is compliant if you see a green check and a compliance note on your workstation's photo. If your workstation is non-compliant, remediate the issue as soon as possible, and check on the individual issues in the Compliance tab and follow the instructions.

Step 3

Throughout your stay in bench, you may be required to install apps that require elevated access. If needed, request a temporary elevated access to proceed with the installation, and make sure that you only install standard Accenture software only.

You can also navigate to this Support topic: Topics > Technology Support > View All Tech > Topics > Privileged Access Management > Privileged Access - Workstation. You can also use Amethyst, and ask it about Admin Rights.
