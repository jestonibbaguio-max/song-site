Updating Contact Details in Workday Guidelines

This is a guide to update your contact details in Workday. Keeping your contact details up to date ensures that you receive important communications and stay connected with your team and organization.

Step 1

Log in to your Workday account and visit profile by clicking View Profile.

Step 2

Look for the Actions panel on the left side of the screen, then click Contact which can be found between Personal and Compensation.

Step 3

Go to the Contact tab and update the necessary information on both Home Contact Information and Work Contact Information.
