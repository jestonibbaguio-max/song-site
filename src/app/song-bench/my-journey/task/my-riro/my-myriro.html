My RiRo Guidelines

Our goal while you're in bench is to make your transition to your next project as smooth as possible.

Step 1
Access the ATCP Bench Portal

Go to: Power Apps → ATCP Bench Portal → RIRO module
Roll-in Checklist(if onboarding to bench/project)
Roll-Off Checklist(if exiting)
This is the official system where RIRO tasks are completed and tracked. Timely completion is mandatory to comply with Bench policies and to avoid escalation ( Required period within 3 days of roll-in )

Step 2
Review All Assigned Items

Carefully go through each checklist item. Do not skip any task.
The checklist ensures full alignmemt with Bench requirements and onboarding/offboarding standards.

Step 3
Update Required Details in Each Item

Provide accurate inputs such as: Status ( Completed/Pending/N/A) for required links or preferences
Incomplete or incorrect entries can delay validation and compliance checks

Step 4
Validate Before Submission

Double check all items are addressed, No missing or blank fields, Links and references are working.
Prevents rework and follow-ups from Bench or leads

Step 5
Submit / Confirm Completion

Complete or acknowledge completion ( If required in the portal )
