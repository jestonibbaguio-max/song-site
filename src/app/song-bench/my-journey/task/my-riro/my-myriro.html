Complete RIRO

Complete your Roll-in/Roll-off(RIRO) Checklist in our repository as well as in the ATCP Bench Portal.

✓ Task Completed
Step 1

Download a RIRO Checklist template from our RIRO repository. Open the designated template, and click File > Create a Copy. You have two choices after this:

  • Create a Copy Online
    You need to rename this file (see Step 2) and move this to RIRO Checklist folder in Sharepoint. To do this: Create a Copy Online > Choose Location > More save locations > [ ... ] (Triple dot button at the middle top of the popup) > RIRO Checklist. Ensure that you don't replace or delete any pre-existing file.
  • Download a Copy
    You need to rename this file. You may complete the RIRO checklist first before uploading this to the Sharepoint.

Sharepoint:↗ RIRO Template

Step 2

Rename your RIRO checklist to the following format: ATCP Digital and Data Bench RIRO - <EID> - RI Date <YYYYMMDD> - RO Date <YYYYMMDD>.

If you don't have a Roll-off date, just leave RO Date as YYYYMMDD.

Step 3

Open the RIRO Checklist template. Make sure to ENABLE MACROS in the popup, then go to the RIRO Dashboard tab and update the following details:

  • Roll-in member: Your EID
  • Employment date: Your Accenture hire date
  • Manila or Cebu hire: Your location if Manila or Cebu
  • Roll-in date: Date rolled into Bench, based on DN
Step 4

Switch to the Roll-in Checklist tab.

Follow the checklist instructions on Column D (References / Guidelines / Links), and edit Column H (Date Completed) if you finish the step by entering the date you finished it. If done correctly, Column I (Computed Status) will auto-fill with the correct status indicating whether the task was completed on time or delayed.

  • Column D: References / Guidelines / Links
  • Column H: Date Completed. Edit this as soon as you complete the task in Column D.
  • Column E: Upload proof, such as a screenshot showing completion or documenting an error.

Some links may not work, so make sure you include a screenshot before marking the task as complete.

Step 5

Once you finish your checklist, upload it to the RIRO Repository.

↗ SharePoint: ATCP Bench RIRO Repository

Step 6

After you have uploaded your RIRO, complete the remaining Roll-in tasks in the ATCP Bench Portal. Ensure that the dashboard displays your information on the homepage, then navigate to the RIRO tab. Follow the instructions there and click Submit once all tasks are marked as complete.

Congratulations on completing the RIRO tasks!
