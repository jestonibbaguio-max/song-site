ATCP Song People Lead Guidelines

People Lead an individual within an organization who is responsible for leading, managing, and developing a group of employees.

Step 1
Identify People Lead

Log in to Workday and open View Profile. You will see who are your People Lead in the Dashboard.

Step 2
Contact People Lead

If the People Lead in the Dashboard are not the right one. Contact your expected People Lead to change the assign to His/Her name.

Step 3
Confirm

Check whether People Lead are now reflected and correctly identify

Step 4
Submit and Keep Your People Lead Updated

An updated people Lead keeps you deployment‑ready and visible for future opportunities.
