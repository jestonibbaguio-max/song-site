Update Priorities in Workday Guidelines

Set clear, actionable priorities in Workday that support your development journey as well as take trainings and required learnings.

As part of our performance goals, all team members are expected to reach minimum proficiency by August 31, 2026:

  • CL10 and above → P3
  • CL11 and below → P2
✓ Task Completed
Step 1

Launch Workday and navigate to Priorities page: Profile > Performance > Priorities Homepage tab.

🗗> Workday

Step 2

On the Priorities Homepage tab, make sure that you have priorities on the four (4) categories:

  • Client Value Creation

    How you create or contribute to success for our clients by delivering 360° value, building and nurturing trusted key client relationships and creating a collaborative and exceptional client experience.

    Example: Maintain a four-day-in-office schedule during bench period.


  • AI Enablement

    How you use AI individually, how you develop your AI skills and those of your team, and how, depending on your role, you embed AI into offerings, assets, and processes at our clients and Accenture.

    Example: Complete all required AI + Agentic AI trainings (including Virtual Workshop Training, TQ Agentic AI)


  • A Great Place to Work for Reinventors

    How you create / contribute to exceptional teams.

    Example: Engage in community and organizational activities by either attending the Town Hall or volunteering for a designated program.


  • Community

    How you make an impact in the areas of corporate citizenship and responsible business, supporting and enhancing the communities in which we work and live.

    Example: Support social innovation inside and outside Accenture, in-person or virtually.
