Update Priorities in Workday Guidelines

We update Workday priorities to ensure our daily tasks align with evolving business goals. Regular updates provide clarity on what we should focus on, help managers track performance for promotions and bonuses, and support ongoing professional development.

Step 1

Open Workday and navigate to the Priorities section.

Step 2

Review your active priorities.

Step 3

Update priority details clearly and accurately.

Step 4

Save all changes in Workday.

Step 5

Confirm the task is now marked complete.
