Register to Percipio, Pluralsight, and Udacity

Access thousands of training materials and build your capability by registering your Accenture account with our learning partners Percipio, Pluralsight, and Udacity.

✓ Task Completed
Step 1

Visit the Percipio learning site and sign-in with your Accenture account by clicking "Accenture Employee" on the SSO page. You will be redirected to Accenture Percipio, where you can take thousands of courses and acquire certifications.

Check the My Learning tab to see your learning assignments.

🗗 Accenture Percipio site
Step 2

Log in to Pluralsight by visiting the site. Click "Login" then "Sign in with company or school (SSO)" and use your Accenture email to sign in. Once successful, you can now take courses, labs, and assessments in Pluralsight.

Accenture Pluralsight access is only on the free tier. Switch to a different learning provider if the training you want to take needs a Paid-tier upgrade.

🗗 Pluralsight site
Step 3

For TQ courses, register or log-in to Udacity. Visit the Udacity site, click Login > Sign-in > Sign-in with your organization. Enter your Accenture email on the address field then hit "Continue". From there, you can search for various courses or go to your dashboard and click "Learning Plan: TQ at Accenture" to start taking required TQ courses.

Udacity offers quality learning materials as well as comprehensive introduction on new tech trends via TQ Learning.
