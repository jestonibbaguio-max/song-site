myCompetency

Your skills represent what you know and what you want to be known for at Accenture. Keeping your skills profile current — updating it each time you roll-off a project or every six months — is the easiest way to showcase your expertise.

Think of your skills as your digital passport, showcasing your strengths and opening doors to meaningful learning and career opportunities. By highlighting what you do best, your skills help connect you to pathways that truly fit your career goals.

Target:

  • CL10 and above → P3
  • CL11 and below → P2
✓ Task Completed
Step 1

Open the myCompetency site and check if you have assigned a Primary and a Secondary skill.

🗗 myCompetency site
Step 2

If you don't have an assigned Primary skill, assign by searching a skill in the site, and or verify if the skill exists by checking the catalog. The assigned Primary skill needs to have an assessment to proceed. If you already have an assigned Primary skill, skip this step.

🗗 ATCP Skill list
Step 3

After assigning a skill, prepare to take the assessment. Follow the important guidelines displayed. You can only attempt one (1) assessment per session so do make sure that you have an uninterrupted network and environment to complete the test. If you already have taken the assessment, you may skip this step.

Step 4

You can retake your primary skill assessment in gap of 2-4-2 weeks until you reach expert (or P3) proficiency.

Step 5

Assign your Secondary Skill in the myCompetency homepage, and take the assessment if available. If you already assigned a Primary skill you are proficient with, and an assigned Secondary skill, your myCompetency journey is complete.
