My Competency Guidelines

MyCompetency allows you to define and assess your primary and secondary skills. Keeping your profile updated helps ensure accurate role matching, deployment readiness, and aligned learning opportunities.

Step 1
Identify your Primary Skill

Log in to My Competency and select your Primary Skill. This represents your main expertise and is used for role matching, deployment readiness, and capability tracking.

Step 2
Add your Secondary Skill(s)

Secondary skills show flexibility and support your primary skill for multi‑skilled project demands.

Step 3
Complete Skill Assessment

Assess your proficiency honestly for each skill based on experience.
Accurate proficiency helps ensure correct staffing, learning recommendations, and career alignment

Step 4
Submit and Keep Your Profile Updated

Submit your assessment and update it when you gain new skills or training.
An updated profile keeps you deployment‑ready and visible for future opportunities.
